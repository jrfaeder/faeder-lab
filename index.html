Modeling how cells process information

We build mathematical models and computational tools to understand how cells sense, process, and respond to information. Our work combines rule-based modeling, stochastic simulation, and statistical inference with close experimental collaborations in immunology, virology, and cell signaling. We develop and maintain BioNetGen, a widely used open-source platform for rule-based modeling, and an open textbook, Rule-Based Modeling of Biological Systems.

Research

From receptor signaling and cytokine networks to viral replication and gene expression, we build mechanistic models that are detailed enough to test against data and make predictions.

Software

BioNetGen, NFsim, PyBioNetGen, BNG Playground, and an open textbook on rule-based modeling. Our software is free, open source, and used by modeling groups around the world.
