Associate Professor in the Department of Computational and Systems Biology at the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine. From 2016 to 2025 he served as co-director of the Joint CMU–Pitt PhD Program in Computational Biology (CPCB) and as the department's Vice Chair for Educational Activities.

He received his A.B. in Chemistry from Harvard University and his Ph.D. in Chemical Physics from the University of Colorado at Boulder, followed by postdoctoral work at Colorado State University and the Weizmann Institute of Science. At Los Alamos National Laboratory (2001–2007), first as a Director's Funded Postdoctoral Fellow and then as a Technical Staff Member in the Theoretical Biology and Biophysics Group, he helped pioneer rule-based modeling of biochemical networks and co-created BioNetGen.
